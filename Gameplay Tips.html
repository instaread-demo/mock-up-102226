Season 1 Gear Progression Guide: 0 to 1800 Gear Score

This guide covers the recommended progression path for reaching 1800 Gear Score (GS) in Season 1, divided into three stages: 0 to 1400 GS, 1400 to 1600 GS, and 1600 to 1800 GS.

0 to 1400 Gear Score

1. Reach Level 45 and Complete the Storyline

  • Reach level 45 by completing the storyline.
  • Collect Abyss Feathers and use them to reach Rank 30. Collect all Abyss Feathers.

2. Complete Regional Quests

Finish all remaining regional quests.

3. Clear Sealed Dungeons and Strongholds

Complete Sealed Dungeons and Strongholds.

4. Complete Nightmare Dungeons

  • Run Nightmare Dungeons.
  • Look for Gatekeeper PinoPi.
  • Buy the Daevanion Crystal.

5. Participate in the Shugo Festival

  • Complete Shugo Festival activities.
  • Buy Stigma Shards.

World Bosses and Hidden Cubes

  • Always participate in the Immortal Gartua world boss encounter.
  • Redeem any Hidden Cube you find.
  • Prioritize the recommended Hidden Cube locations for additional rewards and upgrade materials.

Upgrade Your Gear

Upgrade your gear to +5 or +6 and equip Manastones. These upgrades should help you reach 1400 GS.

1400 to 1600 Gear Score

6. Upgrade Your Equipment

Upgrade all yellow gear to +10 and blue gear to +7.

Run daily dungeons to obtain upgrade materials.

7. Complete Expedition: Exploration in Vakron Sky Land

  • Complete Expedition: Exploration in Vakron Sky Land.
  • Continue until you obtain the reward box.
  • Do not claim the reward yet.

8. Complete Expedition: Conquest in Vakron Sky Land

  • Farm Expedition: Conquest in Vakron Sky Land until you reach 21 runs or obtain 3 to 5 pieces of gear.
  • Claim both reward boxes.

9. Complete the Ascension Trial

Once you reach 1500 GS, complete the Normal Ascension Trial. Skip Easy difficulty.

10. Upgrade Your Pet

  1. Open Genus Insight.
  2. Upgrade Special to Rank 10.
  3. Upgrade all categories to Rank 6.
  4. Continue improving individual categories to Rank 6 to 9, focusing on one category at a time.
  5. Use Hidden Cubes to obtain upgrade materials.

Important: Do not lock any upgrades until the orange ones reach Rank 9. Only start locking the orange ones at Rank 9.

1600 to 1800 Gear Score

11. Complete Transcendence Stage 1

  • Complete Stage 1 of Transcendence.
  • Do both activities twice.
  • Equip the Arcane Cards.

12. Farm Expedition: Conquest in Urugugu

  • Run Expedition: Conquest in Urugugu until you reach 21 runs, if needed.
  • Continue farming until you have enough gear to reach 1800 GS.

Additional Tips and Daily Activities

Command Shop

Check the Command Shop in towns. Roll 4 to 5 times at most.

Shugo Festival Achievements

Complete all Shugo Festival events three times to earn achievements and rewards.

World Bosses and Titles

Defeat all world bosses to obtain titles that provide passive stats.

Remember to equip your titles and wings, as they provide stats.

Supply Requests

Always check Supply Requests and complete them.

Odyle

Whenever you find Odyle, collect it if possible.

Draupnir Runs and the Powerful Statue

  • Complete Draupnir eight times before the reset without claiming the rewards.
  • After the reset, complete two additional runs without claiming the rewards.
  • Following this sequence will grant you a powerful statue.

Final Checklist

  • Reach level 45 and complete the storyline.
  • Collect all Abyss Feathers and reach Rank 30.
  • Complete all remaining regional quests, Sealed Dungeons, and Strongholds.
  • Run Nightmare Dungeons, look for Gatekeeper PinoPi, and buy the Daevanion Crystal.
  • Participate in the Shugo Festival and buy Stigma Shards.
  • Defeat Immortal Gartua and redeem Hidden Cubes.
  • Upgrade gear to +5 or +6 and equip Manastones.
  • Upgrade yellow gear to +10 and blue gear to +7.
  • Run daily dungeons for upgrade materials.
  • Complete the Vakron Sky Land Exploration and Conquest activities, following the reward-claiming instructions.
  • Complete the Normal Ascension Trial at 1500 GS.
  • Upgrade Genus Insight, including Special to Rank 10, and improve the other categories.
  • Complete Transcendence Stage 1 and equip the Arcane Cards.
  • Farm Expedition: Conquest in Urugugu until you reach 1800 GS.
  • Complete Shugo Festival events three times for achievements and rewards.
  • Defeat world bosses for titles and passive stats.
  • Equip titles and wings.
  • Check Supply Requests and collect Odyle when possible.
  • Complete eight Draupnir runs before the reset and two after the reset without claiming the rewards.

Good luck, and happy hunting, Elyos!
